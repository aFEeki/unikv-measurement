LEAD WITH THE FAILURE: gamma is not model-independent
-------------------------------------------------------
The paper argues gamma is a graph-shape and tier-handoff cost and should
therefore be roughly the same on any model. Predicted ~8.8 ms CPU-pinned and
~2.7 ms device-visible. Measured:

  gamma                  Llama      Qwen     ratio    predicted
  CPU-pinned [means]     8.762 ms   4.119 ms  0.470      1.0     FAILS
  CPU-pinned [medians]   8.640 ms   5.216 ms  0.604      1.0     FAILS
  device-vis [means]     2.708 ms   1.918 ms  0.708      1.0     FAILS
  device-vis [medians]   2.656 ms   1.906 ms  0.718      1.0     FAILS

gamma falls 30% on the device-visible tier and 40-53% on the CPU-pinned tier.
The direction is informative: the CPU-pinned gamma ratio (0.470) is close to the
KV-bytes ratio (0.4375). gamma carries model-dependent work — plausibly a
per-layer or per-byte staging cost paid once on entry to the two-tier path —
that the paper books as a fixed property of the graph. Either measure what
gamma is made of, or stop calling it model-independent.

THE HEADLINE QUESTION: what is delta made of?
-----------------------------------------------
Finding 1 admits it cannot separate bandwidth-to-the-pages from per-cell kernel
overhead. Predicted delta ratio: 0.4375 if bytes, 1.0 if per-cell overhead.

  delta                  Llama        Qwen              ratio   verdict
  CPU-pinned [means]     3.0990       3.5779 +/-0.4135  1.155   bytes REJECTED (t=5.4)
                                                                unchanged: consistent (t=1.1)
  CPU-pinned [medians]   2.8531       2.6667 +/-0.2136  0.935   bytes REJECTED (t=6.6)
                                                                unchanged: consistent (t=-0.9)
  device-vis [means]     2.1531       1.7174 +/-0.0058  0.798   BOTH REJECTED
                                                                (bytes t=133, unchanged t=-55)
  device-vis [medians]   2.1549       1.7184 +/-0.0062  0.797   BOTH REJECTED

THE ANSWER IS TIER-DEPENDENT, and that is the finding:

  CPU-PINNED: delta does not track bytes. It is consistent with being unchanged
  across a 2.3x change in KV per cell, on both estimators. Charged per CELL.

  DEVICE-VISIBLE: delta rejects both pure hypotheses and sits strictly between
  them at 0.798 +/- 0.003. Solving delta = A + B x (KiB per cell) over the two
  blocks gives A = 1.379 us/cell size-independent and B = 6.05 ns/cell/KiB, so
  36.0% of Llama's device-visible delta is a bytes term and 64.0% is not.

  TWO CAVEATS THAT LIMIT THIS, BOTH REAL:
  (a) With two models, A and B are two equations in two unknowns — no residual,
      no degrees of freedom. That is a description consistent with both blocks,
      not a fitted law. What is established is the REJECTION of both
      one-parameter predictions, which is estimator-stable and huge in t.
  (b) The models differ in more than bytes. KV/cell = 0.4375 factors as
      (28/32) x (512/1024) = layers x width. A purely PER-LAYER cost predicts
      0.875. The measured 0.798 +/- 0.003 rejects that too (t = 23) but lies far
      closer to it than to 0.4375. Two models cannot separate a per-layer term
      from a per-byte term because both move together. A THIRD MODEL WITH
      LLAMA'S LAYER COUNT AND QWEN'S KV WIDTH (or the reverse) WOULD SEPARATE
      THEM, and that is the specific experiment this result motivates.

THE SCRATCH FORMULA: PREDICTS, does not merely describe
---------------------------------------------------------
Scratch is modelled as n_kv x n_ubatch x n_head, so per context token it should
scale with head count: predicted ratio 28/32 = 0.875.

  scratch KiB/ctx-token   Llama    Qwen     ratio    predicted
  ub 512                  66.42    58.25    0.877      0.875
  ub 256                  33.21    29.06    0.875      0.875

  context ceiling         Llama    Qwen              predicted (Qwen)
  ub 512                  49152    98304-114688      ~109,000   inside bracket
  ub 256                  65536    131072-147456     ~145,000   inside bracket

Qwen COMPLETES at C=65536 at ub512, the configuration where Llama is refused.
Both refusals are the execution-time Metal refusal at 1.035x and 1.002x of the
advisory budget, ~1.2 s in — Finding 4's mechanism, reproduced.

The property is now a two-architecture claim. The INDEX is model-specific and
should never be quoted as a property of the method — note also that the
prompt ordering flips (prose diverges earlier than passkey on Llama, later on
Qwen), so the "high-entropy prompts are harder to reproduce" reading suggested
by the Llama block does not survive a second model.

WHAT A REVIEWER GETS FROM THIS THAT THEY DID NOT HAVE
-------------------------------------------------------
The one-model weakness is now answered on the axis that pays: three of the
paper's formulas were tested predictively rather than replicated. Two survived
(scratch, ceiling), one failed (gamma model-independence), and the contested one
(delta's mechanism) resolved into a tier-dependent answer the paper could not
previously state. The remaining honest gap is the layer/width confound, which is
one more model away and is now specified precisely enough to run.
