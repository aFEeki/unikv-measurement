WHY THIS BLOCK EXISTS
---------------------
Section 8 gives the exactness premium (11.4% over H2O at C=1024) and Section 3
gives the fused-kernel penalty (12.4% at C=4096) from DIFFERENT blocks, and the
paper declines to compose them because that is exactly the splice Section 6
refuses on its own data. All four arms in one cooled block removes the need to
decline.

PREFLIGHT — recorded, not asserted
------------------------------------
The paper states that policy 3 cannot use the fused kernel. Rather than assert it
from the source, the harness tries it and records what happens:

  policy 3 device-visible, -fa on : rc=1, flash_attn=enabled, decoded=None
                                    "failed to create context with model"
  policy 4 H2O,            -fa on : rc=1, flash_attn=enabled, decoded=None
                                    "failed to create context with model"

Both REFUSE TO CONSTRUCT. Note the runtime reports flash_attn=enabled before
failing, i.e. the refusal is ours at context construction, not the backend
declining the kernel. This is why the composition below is necessary at all:
there is no way to measure either policy against itself with the fused kernel.

PROTOCOL
--------
Randomised COMPLETE block: three rounds, each a random permutation of all eight
(arm, ctx) cells, so arm is orthogonal to block position by construction.
Position audit, mean slot: p0_fa_on 10.67, p0_fa_off 12.17, p4_h2o 13.83,
p3_dev 13.33.

512-token prompt, 2048 decode budget, C in {1024, 2048}, -b/-ub 512, greedy,
seed 123, EOS disabled, uninstrumented, 200 s cooldowns. flash_attn parsed back
from every run's own log and asserted against what the arm requested: "enabled"
on all six p0_fa_on runs, "disabled" on the other eighteen. All rc=0. No
exclusions, no flags.

(a) THE KERNEL PENALTY AT THESE BUDGETS — IT DOES NOT CARRY DOWN
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Same policy, same halting behaviour, same decoded length, only the kernel
differs. This is the clean contrast and the brief asked for it explicitly
because the published 12.4% is from C=4096.

    C=1024:  46.624 vs 42.842  ->  fused is  +8.83% faster
    C=2048:  45.989 vs 40.967  ->  fused is +12.26% faster
    C=4096:  published                       +12.4%

The penalty is essentially unchanged from C=2048 to C=4096 (12.26% vs 12.4%) and
markedly SMALLER at C=1024 (8.83%, about 30% less). So the published figure
carries at C=2048 and above and must not be quoted at C=1024. That is the
expected direction — with the explicit softmax the materialised KQ matrix scales
with the resident cache, so its relative cost grows with C and saturates once it
dominates — but it was previously assumed rather than measured at the small
budget where the paper's headline comparisons live.

(b) THE EXACTNESS PREMIUM — REPLICATES TO 0.15 PERCENTAGE POINTS
------------------------------------------------------------------
Both arms -fa off, same block, both decoding the full 2048.

    C=1024:  p3_dev 37.626 vs h2o 41.869  ->  +11.28%
             (b2 block: +11.42%   paper: 11.4%)
    C=2048:  p3_dev 39.119 vs h2o 39.635  ->   +1.32%
             (b2 block: +1.33%)

An independent cooled block reproduces both published premia to within 0.15 and
0.01 percentage points. Section 8's numbers are solid.

(c) THE COMPOSED NUMBER — WHAT EXACT RETENTION COSTS AGAINST A FUSED BASELINE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two routes, and they are not equivalent.

  DIRECT (p3_dev vs p0_fa_on):      C=1024 +23.91%    C=2048 +17.56%
  COMPOSED (premium x penalty):     C=1024 +21.10%    C=2048 +13.74%

THE DIRECT ROUTE IS AN UPPER BOUND AND SHOULD BE LABELLED ONE. Policy 0 halts at
cache-full: at C=1024 it decodes 512 tokens and stops, at C=2048 it decodes 1536,
while p3_dev decodes the full 2048. So the baseline is timed only over the
regime where the cache is not yet full, the KV is small and no spilled tier
exists — the cheapest part of the run — while the retention arm is timed over a
regime that includes recall from the spilled tier. The decoded counts are in the
CSV per run so this is visible rather than buried.

The gap between the routes is not a modelling choice; it is exactly one
measurable quantity. Direct / composed equals p0_fa_off / p4_h2o, to six decimal
places, at both budgets:

    C=1024:  1.0232 = 1.0232        C=2048:  1.0336 = 1.0336

i.e. the two routes differ by precisely the amount that upstream-while-halting
outruns H2O-while-completing (2.3% and 3.4%). That is the halting artifact,
isolated and quantified, and it is the reason to prefer the composed route.

THE COMPOSED ROUTE'S OWN ASSUMPTION, STATED PLAINLY: it multiplies the premium
over H2O by the kernel penalty measured ON POLICY 0, because neither H2O nor
policy 3 can run the fused kernel at all (see PREFLIGHT). It therefore assumes
the kernel penalty transfers from a non-evicting policy to an evicting one. That
assumption is unavoidable given the refusal and it is not tested here. It is
weaker than a splice across blocks — everything is measured in this one block —
but it is not free.

WHAT THE PAPER CAN NOW STATE
-----------------------------
"Within a single cooled block, exact retention on the device-visible tier costs
21.1% against an upstream baseline running the fused kernel at C=1024, and 13.7%
at C=2048, composing an 11.3% premium over H2O with an 8.8% kernel penalty
measured on the same policy in the same block. A direct comparison against
upstream-with-fusion gives 23.9% and 17.6%, but upstream halts at cache-full and
is therefore timed only over the not-yet-full regime; the difference between the
two figures is exactly the margin by which halting upstream outruns completing
H2O."

That is the number the paper currently cannot state, and it no longer requires
the splice Section 6 refuses.

LIMITS
------
- One model, one machine, one prompt length, two budgets, three trials.
- The composed route assumes the fused-kernel penalty transfers across policies.
  Untestable here because policies 3 and 4 refuse to construct with -fa on; a
  fused-compatible eviction policy would be needed to check it.
- The direct route's halting asymmetry is quantified above but not removed. A
  baseline that completes 2048 tokens with the fused kernel does not exist in
  this fork, which is the whole difficulty.
- All figures are throughput ratios on a 512-token prompt. They are not claims
  about long-context behaviour.
