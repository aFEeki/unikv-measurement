STRUCTURE (for framing / Fig 4):
- Linear fit on the alpha>0 points: decode_ms = 69549 + 3118*alpha (ms).
- Measured alpha=0 = 69019 ms sits 530 ms BELOW that extrapolated intercept.
  That 530 ms is the physical non-blocking->blocking transition: alpha=0 has no
  per-step synchronize() (natural GPU/CPU overlap); alpha>0 drains each spilling
  step. Small and clean -- correct earlier over-estimate (the A/B verify implied
  ~2.2 s only because it assumed the idealized 2417 ms/alpha slope; with 6 alpha>0
  points the measured slope is 3118 ms/alpha and the true offset is 530 ms).
- Idealized transfer term (analytic, sum(n_spilled)*per_cell/64GB/s) = 2417
  ms/alpha; measured slope 3118 ms/alpha. The ~29% excess is per-step overhead
  (synchronize drain + nanosleep granularity over ~1536 spilling steps), i.e. real
  blocking-recall cost beyond the idealized byte transfer. Fig 4 can show measured
  wall-clock (3118) or the idealized transfer term (2417) -- both recoverable.

READ FOR THE PAPER: alpha=0 (unified memory) is the fast anchor; as alpha rises
toward the A100 PCIe point (alpha=1) and beyond, throughput falls smoothly and
resolvably. "From PCIe to Zero" made visible: the transfer term vanishes at
alpha=0 and grows monotonically with modeled transfer cost.
