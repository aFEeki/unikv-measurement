WHY IT IS FLAT — signal below noise, not a bug:
1. Thermal drift this session is MONOTONIC (heat-soaked M4), not the §3 U-shape:
   tok/s vs run-position slope = -0.072 /run => -2.5 tok/s across the sweep;
   first-5-runs mean 25.44 vs last-5 21.38. Monotonic drift is NOT cancelled by
   block-averaging the way a U-shape is.
2. This seed anti-correlated alpha with run position: mean run-position by alpha
   = {a0:18.6, a0.25:16.6, a0.5:25.0, a0.75:19.8, a1:21.6, a1.5:13.4, a2:11.0}.
   High-alpha ran earlier/cooler, low/mid-alpha later/hotter -> the thermal
   penalty partly offsets the alpha penalty in raw means.
3. The expected alpha effect is genuinely SMALL. Injected delay is additive:
   K = sum_over_steps(n_spilled) * per_cell_bytes / 64 GB/s
     = (1536*1537/2 cells) * 128 KiB / 64e9 = 2414 ms per unit-alpha.
   So alpha=1 adds ~2.41 s on a ~72 s base decode = ~3.4%; alpha=2 ~6.7%.
   That is BELOW the thermal noise (std ~6%, drift ~11%) on this device.

Position-detrended (linear) residual by alpha still noisy/non-monotonic
  (a0 -0.42, a0.25 -0.12, a0.5 +0.94, a0.75 +0.88, a1 -0.29, a1.5 +0.30,
   a2 -1.28): a faint hint (a2 lowest) but not a clean trend, because the drift
  is not perfectly linear and the effect is small.

INTERPRETATION: the recall-cost delay at unified-memory-realistic calibration is
small by design (recall is nearly free -- the thesis), and a heat-soaked
sequential sweep cannot resolve a ~3.4%/unit effect against ~11% thermal drift.
This is NOT the same situation as the old policy-1 sweep, whose modeled cost was
larger per event.

INJECTION VERIFIED (cooldown-paired A/B, 200s idles, injection_verify.csv):
  alpha=0: 69045, 69039, 69141 ms  mean 69075  (std ~50 ms)
  alpha=2: 75984, 76217, 76098 ms  mean 76100  (std ~120 ms)
  delta = +7025 ms vs +4834 ms idealized sleep. The ~2.2 s excess is real
  blocking-regime overhead (per-step synchronize() drain + nanosleep granularity
  over ~1536 spilling steps), i.e. the non-blocking->blocking transition, not a
  bug. In tok/s: alpha=0 29.65 -> alpha=2 26.91, clean monotone ~1.37 tok/s per
  unit-alpha (~4.6%). CONCLUSION: the delay lands correctly; the flat 35-run
  sweep was purely thermal masking. With 200 s cooldowns run-to-run variance
  drops from +-1.5 tok/s to +-0.05 tok/s and the trend is cleanly resolvable
  RIGHT NOW (no need for a cold device).
