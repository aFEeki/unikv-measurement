TWO THINGS FOR JUDGMENT'S MODEL DECISION (before the full sweep):

1. Per-step synchronize() only fires at alpha>0. At alpha=0 the whole block is
   skipped, so there is no per-step drain and the GPU/CPU pipeline overlaps

   This can put the alpha=0 point slightly OFF the line the alpha>0 points form
   (a possible discontinuity at 0). Physically defensible (a real PCIe recall is
   a blocking dependency that breaks overlap; unified memory has no such stall),
   but it is a framing choice: fit the line on alpha>0 and show alpha=0 as the
   "unified-memory / no modeled transfer" reference, OR redefine the gate to sync
   every step including alpha=0. I did NOT change the injection — this is yours.
