THE PREDICTION, STATED BEFORE THE RUN
---------------------------------------
The paper models attention scratch as n_kv x n_ubatch x n_head, so per token of
context it should scale with the HEAD count, independently of the KV geometry
that drives everything else in this night's work. Qwen has 28 heads against
Llama's 32:

  predicted scratch ratio = 28/32 = 0.875

and with a smaller KV term as well (56.0 vs 128.0 KiB/cell), a correspondingly
higher context ceiling. Predicted per-context-token device cost and ceiling,
computed from the parsed geometry and the model's own reported buffer sizes
(Qwen model = 4168.09 + 292.36 = 4460.45 MiB; advisory budget 16383 MiB):

  ub512:  56.0 KV + 56.0 scratch = 112.0 KiB/tok  ->  ceiling ~109,000 tokens
  ub256:  56.0 KV + 28.0 scratch =  84.0 KiB/tok  ->  ceiling ~145,000 tokens

Llama's MEASURED ceilings for comparison: 49152 at ub512 (65536 refused),
65536 at ub256 (81920 refused).

1. THE SCRATCH COEFFICIENT MATCHES THE FORMULA
   measured   Qwen 58.25 / Llama 66.42 KiB/tok at ub512  ->  ratio 0.877
   measured   Qwen 29.06 / Llama 33.21 KiB/tok at ub256  ->  ratio 0.875
   predicted  28/32                                      =        0.875

   Both ubatch settings reproduce the head-count ratio, one of them exactly. The
   n_kv x n_ubatch x n_head model of the scratch term is confirmed on a second
   architecture. (Each model also carries a small constant above the pure
   formula — Llama 66.42 against a predicted 64.0, Qwen 58.25 against 56.0, both
   about +2.2 KiB/tok — which is why the ratio is 0.877 rather than 0.875 at
   ub512. The constant is the same size on both models, so it does not scale
   with heads and is not part of the term being tested.)

2. THE CEILING MOVES AS PREDICTED, AND THE HEADLINE IS BINARY
   At ub512 Qwen COMPLETES at C=65536 — the configuration at which Llama is
   refused by the driver — and again at C=98304, failing only at 114688. Its
   ceiling is bracketed between 98304 and 114688 against a prediction of
   ~109,000, i.e. the prediction lands inside the measured bracket.
   At ub256 the ceiling is bracketed between 131072 and 147456 against a
   prediction of ~145,000, again inside the bracket.

   So the same machine, same runtime and same flags reach a 2.0x higher context
   ceiling on a model with 0.44x the KV per cell and 0.875x the heads, and the
   paper's own arithmetic predicts where.

3. THE FAILURE MODE REPLICATES
   Both refusals are the execution-time Metal refusal, not an allocation
   failure: they occur at 1.035x and 1.002x of the advisory budget after the
   context loads successfully, and they take ~1.2 s against ~53 s for a
   completing run — i.e. they fail on the first ubatch of the prompt. This is
   Finding 4's mechanism reproduced on a second model: the limit appears at
   execution, and the boundary sits where the arithmetic says it should.

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE PAPER
-------------------------------
Finding 4's per-token accounting (193 KiB/token for Llama: 128 KV + 65 scratch)
currently reads as a measurement of one model. It is a formula, and this is the
first evidence that it predicts a different model's ceiling rather than merely
describing the one it was fitted on. That is a stronger claim than the paper
makes, and it costs one table.

It also sharpens the "why not simply enlarge the cache?" argument: the ceiling
is not a fixed property of the hardware, it is (budget - model) / (KV + scratch)
per token, and a model with narrower KV geometry buys context ceiling roughly in
proportion. On this machine that is the difference between 49k and ~109k tokens
at the same ubatch.

LIMITS
------
- Two ubatch settings on the new model, one on each side of the Llama pair.
- The ceilings are brackets, not points; the walk steps in 16384-token
  increments, so "between 98304 and 114688" is as precise as this ladder gets.
- Qwen's n_ctx_train is 32768, so the large-C runs are far outside the model's
  trained context. That is irrelevant to a capacity test — the outcome is
  whether the driver executes the command buffer, not whether the output is
  sensible — but it means these configurations are not useful operating points
  and must not be presented as though they were.
- flash attention off throughout, parsed back per run, matching the spill path
  and the Llama sweep.
