BLOCK 1 — isochronal recall cost, both modes
===========================================================================
Per-step cost at controlled spilled-set size, C=1024, burst of 128 decode steps
with the first 32 discarded (the one-off graph-realloc step after prefill).
Instrumented by necessity: per-step wall clock IS the measurement. n=3 per
point, except CPU-pinned n_spill=4096 which got 5.

  n_spill    CPU-pinned ms/step      device-visible ms/step
        0      22.554 +/- 0.034        22.544 +/- 0.052
      512      33.958 +/- 0.085        26.552 +/- 0.064
     1024      34.702 +/- 0.153        27.639 +/- 0.063
     2048      37.344 +/- 0.200        29.773 +/- 0.077
     4096      43.892 +/- 0.696 (n=5)  34.264 +/- 0.021
     8192      57.349 +/- 1.456        43.063 +/- 0.028

  CPU-pinned      t = 31.563 ms + 3.0990 us/cell   SE 0.0764, resid SD 0.832
                  gamma = 31.563 - 22.554 = 9.009 ms
  device-visible  t = 25.423 ms + 2.1531 us/cell   SE 0.0055, resid SD 0.059
                  gamma = 25.423 - 22.544 = 2.879 ms

Q1 — DO THE NO-SPILL REFERENCES AGREE?  YES.
  22.554 vs 22.544 ms, difference -0.010 ms, SE 0.036, t = -0.28.
  With nothing spilled the tier is inert and the two modes are indistinguishable.
  The buffer allocation does nothing at idle, so the modes are cleanly
  comparable and the gamma/delta differences below are not an allocation
  artefact.

Q2 — ARE THE TWO SLOPES SEPARABLE?  YES, DECISIVELY.
  delta CPU-pinned     = 3.0990 +/- 0.0764 us/cell
  delta device-visible = 2.1531 +/- 0.0055 us/cell
  difference           = 0.9459 +/- 0.0766 us/cell,  t = 12.34
  Device-visible delta is 30.5% lower. This survives the test comfortably, so
  the difference can be reported as measured rather than as a direction.

WHAT THE TWO TERMS SAY
  gamma  9.009 -> 2.879 ms, a 68% fall. The fixed term is a handoff cost, and
         removing the CPU handoff removes about two thirds of it. What remains
         (2.879 ms) is the cost of the two-tier graph shape itself -- the
         separate score matmul, the concat, the union softmax -- which does not
         go away by moving processors.
  delta  3.0990 -> 2.1531 us/cell, a 30.5% fall. This is the half of the
         prediction that was wrong: delta is NOT a location-independent data
         term. Streaming the spilled tier is measurably cheaper on the GPU,
         which has more bandwidth to the same physical pages. On unified memory
         even the data term depends on which processor touches the data.

THE n_spill=4096 ANOMALY IS RESOLVED
  Earlier sweep: 5 trials spanning 44.0-60.9 ms, sd 7.0, where every other point
  repeated under 0.5 ms. Re-run on a quiet machine: 43.892 +/- 0.696, and the
  mean sits at the bottom of the old range. It was background machine activity.
  The mystery is deleted, not confirmed, and the point now carries no more
  weight than its neighbours.

REPRODUCIBILITY ACROSS NIGHTS (CPU-pinned, vs the earlier protocol sweep)
  gamma   9.03  -> 9.009 ms
  delta   3.289 +/- 0.086 -> 3.0990 +/- 0.0764 us/cell
  The affine model reproduces on a different night with a rebuilt binary.

PRECISION IS ITSELF A RESULT
  Residual SD 0.832 ms (CPU-pinned) against 0.059 ms (device-visible), a factor
  of 14. And CPU-pinned scatter GROWS with the spilled set (sd 0.085 at 512 ->
  1.456 at 8192) while device-visible stays flat (0.064 -> 0.028). The
  CPU-pinned path is not merely slower; its cost is less predictable, and
  increasingly so as the tier grows. Same processor-dependent throttling the
  interleaved A/B showed, now visible inside the cost curve.

BLOCK 2 — policy comparison, five arms
===========================================================================
512-token prompt + 2048-token decode budget, uninstrumented. p3_cpu
position-balanced at slots 2, 10, 14, 16, 22, 28 of 30 (one per sixth); all
other arms randomised.

  arm                        C=1024                C=2048
  upstream (halts)      43.39 +/- 0.04  (512)  41.51 +/- 0.03  (1536)
  rolling window        39.40 +/- 0.16 (2048)  39.38 +/- 0.01 (2048)
  H2O                   42.42 +/- 0.01 (2048)  40.17 +/- 0.04 (2048)
  exact, CPU-pinned     30.24 +/- 0.15 (2048)  35.62 +/- 0.25 (2048)
  exact, device-visible 38.07 +/- 0.01 (2048)  39.64 +/- 0.01 (2048)

EXACTNESS PREMIUM (how much faster the lossy comparator is)
                        vs H2O        vs rolling window
  C=1024  CPU-pinned    +40.26%       +30.27%
  C=1024  device-visible +11.42%       +3.48%
  C=2048  CPU-pinned    +12.77%       +10.54%
  C=2048  device-visible  +1.33%       -0.67%

  At C=2048 with a device-visible tier, exact retention is within 1.3% of H2O
  and marginally FASTER than the rolling window (39.64 vs 39.38). Exactness is
  close to free at that budget. At C=1024 it costs 11.4% against H2O.

  The premium therefore depends strongly on how much is spilled: 1535 cells at
  C=1024 against 511 at C=2048. That is the same scaling Block 1 measures
  directly, and the two blocks agree.

POSITION AUDIT OF THE BALANCED ARM
  slot2=35.38 (C=2048)   slot10=30.07 (C=1024)  slot14=35.88 (C=2048)
  slot16=30.28 (C=1024)  slot22=35.61 (C=2048)  slot28=30.37 (C=1024)
  Values cluster by CONTEXT, not by slot: the three C=2048 runs (slots 2, 14,
  22) span 35.38-35.88 and the three C=1024 runs (slots 10, 16, 28) span
  30.07-30.37, with no trend across the block. The balancing worked and shows
  no thermal-position effect to correct for.

===========================================================================
WHY THE INTERLEAVED A/B DISAGREED, AND WHAT IT MEANS FOR METHOD
===========================================================================
                          A/B (uncooled)   B2 (protocol)
  p3_cpu                        25.22          30.24   (+19.9%)
  p3_dev                        36.60          38.07   ( +4.0%)
  p4_h2o                        39.45          42.42   ( +7.5%)
  premium H2O over p3_dev       +7.77%        +11.42%
  speedup p3_dev over p3_cpu   +46.50%        +25.89%

Every arm is faster on a cool quiet machine, but by very different amounts, and
the CPU-pinned arm gains most (+19.9%) because it was the most degraded. That
biases the two headline comparisons in OPPOSITE directions: the uncooled
session overstated the device-visible speedup (46.5% vs 25.9%) and understated
the exactness premium (7.8% vs 11.4%).

The methodological point is worth a sentence in the paper. Counterbalancing
protects against ORDER effects within a session; it does not protect against an
arm being systematically more sensitive to the session's overall thermal LEVEL.
When arms differ in which processor they load, only a cooled block gives
comparable numbers -- interleaving is not a substitute for it, merely a cheaper
guard against a different failure.

Note also that the original B1 smoke (+24% for device-visible over CPU-pinned)
was closer to the protocol value (+25.9%) than the more carefully designed
interleaved A/B (+46.5%) was. That is not an argument for smoke; it is a
reminder that a single lucky comparison is not evidence, and that the A/B's
error was systematic rather than random -- which is exactly the kind a larger n
does not fix.

===========================================================================
LIMITS
===========================================================================
- n=3 per point (5 at CPU-pinned n_spill=4096). SDs are small but this is one
  machine on one night.
- Block 1 is instrumented; its ms/step values carry the per-step synchronize()
  pair and are NOT comparable to Block 2's uninstrumented tok/s. The two blocks
  are internally consistent, not cross-comparable.
- Block 1 fits are over n_spill <= 8192. The earlier finding that the cost turns
  superlinear beyond ~10^4 cells (measured at 61471) was made in CPU-pinned mode
  and has NOT been re-measured device-visible; do not extrapolate either fit
  past its range.
- The device-visible mode's throughput advantage comes with the bounded-device
  property removed (B1: charged at spill capacity, ceiling 81920-90112 cells).
  Neither block re-measures that, and no throughput number here should be quoted
  without it.
- One model, one quantisation, one device, ubatch 512.
