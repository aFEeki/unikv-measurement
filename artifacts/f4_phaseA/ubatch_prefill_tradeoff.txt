The ubatch/capacity trade — the paper asserts a cost the data does not show
================================================================================
2026-08-11. NO NEW RUNS. Re-analysis of stress_results/f4_a1_ubatch_sweep.csv
(the F4 Phase A1 sweep, harness scripts/run_f4_phaseA.py, logs in
artifacts/f4_phaseA/logs/).

Prompted by the review note that the paper says lowering ubatch buys capacity
but costs prefill parallelism, and leaves the cost unquantified. Checked before
running anything, as instructed. Nothing needs to be run: the A1 sweep already
measured prefill time at four ubatch settings, and it does not support the
sentence as written.

THE CLAIM
---------
Reducing ubatch shrinks the attention scratch buffer, which raises the maximum
workable context; the paper concedes this "at a prefill-throughput cost" without
saying how much. (The concession originates in artifacts/capacity_probe/
capacity_analysis.txt, LIMITS, which flagged the sweep as not yet run. It has
since been run.)

WHAT THE SWEEP MEASURED
-----------------------
Policy 0, 16384-token prompt, 8 decoded tokens, -fa off (parsed as "disabled" on
every run), -b 512 throughout, ubatch varied. Prefill throughput below is
16384 / prefill_ms, computed here from the CSV's own prefill_ms column.

  ub   C=49152        C=65536        C=81920        C=98304
  64   259.6 tok/s    256.2 tok/s    250.0 tok/s    GPU_OOM
 128   259.4          253.9           65.8 (*)      GPU_OOM
 256   268.2          258.0          GPU_OOM         --
 512   237.0          GPU_OOM         --             --

  (*) not a ubatch effect — see below.

FINDING 1: THE CAPACITY GAIN IS REAL AND LARGE
-----------------------------------------------
Maximum workable context by ubatch: 81920 at ub64 and ub128, 65536 at ub256,
49152 at ub512. Dropping ubatch from 512 to 64 raises the ceiling by 1.67x
(49152 -> 81920 tokens), because the scratch term falls from 66.4 to 8.3 KiB per
token of context — an 8x reduction in the C-scaled part of the device working
set. That half of the paper's sentence is well supported.

FINDING 2: THE PREFILL COST IS NOT VISIBLE IN THIS DATA
--------------------------------------------------------
At the one context all four settings can run (C=49152), prefill throughput is
259.6 / 259.4 / 268.2 / 237.0 tok/s for ub 64/128/256/512. It is flat from 64 to
256 — a 3.3% spread with no monotone trend — and the SLOWEST setting is the
largest ubatch, which is the opposite of the predicted direction. The same
pattern holds at C=65536 (256.2 / 253.9 / 258.0 across ub 64/128/256, a 1.6%
spread).

So on this machine, over ub 64-512 at a 16k prompt, reducing ubatch does not
measurably cost prefill throughput.

The likely reason is that prefill here is not parallelism-limited. With flash
attention off the KQ matrix is materialised at n_kv x n_ubatch, so a larger
ubatch buys wider GEMMs but proportionally more scratch traffic through unified
memory; on an M4 Pro the bandwidth term appears to cancel the parallelism term
over this range. That is an interpretation, not a measurement — the measurement
is only that the difference is not there.

WHY THE ub128/C=81920 OUTLIER IS NOT A COUNTEREXAMPLE
------------------------------------------------------
That cell reads 65.8 tok/s, 3.9x slower than everything else. It is not a ubatch
effect: the same ub128 gives 259.4 and 253.9 tok/s at C=49152 and C=65536. It is
the memory-pressure degradation at 0.946x of the advisory budget — the last
configuration before the GPU_OOM cliff, where the working set is close enough to
the limit that the system is paging rather than refusing. ub64 at the same C sits
at 0.907x and runs at full speed. The cliff, not ubatch, explains it.

This is worth a sentence in the paper on its own: between "completes at full
speed" and "refuses to execute" there is a narrow band, somewhere between 0.907x
and 1.02x of the advisory budget, where a configuration completes at roughly a
quarter speed. F4 currently presents the boundary as a clean cliff. It is a cliff
with one soft step in front of it, observed once.

WHAT THE PAPER SHOULD SAY
--------------------------
Not "at a prefill-throughput cost" but something like: "on this machine the
capacity gain is not offset by a measurable prefill penalty — over ubatch 64-512
at a 16k prompt, prefill throughput varies by 3.3% with no monotone trend."

LIMITS — WHY THIS IS A NEGATIVE RESULT AND NOT A POSITIVE ONE
--------------------------------------------------------------
These are n=1, uncooled, single-trial runs from a capacity sweep whose purpose
was the OOM boundary, not throughput. The 237-268 tok/s spread is within the
thermal variation seen elsewhere in this project across uncooled runs. So:

  - "no measurable prefill penalty" is supported;
  - "ub512 is 12% slower than ub256" is NOT supported by these data, and should
    not be claimed either, even though it is the direction the numbers point;
  - a cooled block would be needed to claim any ubatch ordering as real.

The honest statement is that the paper currently asserts a cost it never
measured, and the data it does have shows no such cost at the resolution
available. Replacing an unmeasured assertion with a measured non-effect plus its
resolution limit is a strict improvement; running a cooled ubatch block to
resolve differences of a few percent would be a larger expenditure than the
claim is worth, since the sentence's purpose is only to concede that "use a
smaller ubatch" is a partial counter to Finding 5.
