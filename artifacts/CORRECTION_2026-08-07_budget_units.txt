CORRECTION — MB vs MiB in every "x budget" ratio
================================================
Found 2026-08-07 while building the optional capacity figure. Affects ratios
only. No measured quantity changes and no conclusion changes.

WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
ggml prints the Metal budget in DECIMAL megabytes:

    ggml_metal_device_init: recommendedMaxWorkingSetSize  = 17179.89 MB

Every footprint we record -- KV buffer, compute buffer, model buffer, the
memory_breakdown row -- is in MiB. 17179.89 MB is 16384.0 MiB exactly
(17179.89e6 / 2^20). Several harnesses divided a MiB total by 17179.89 as if it
were MiB, so those ratios came out 4.9% too low.

Affected code (now fixed, all convert MB -> MiB before dividing):
    scripts/run_capacity_probe.py      -> capacity_probe.csv
    scripts/run_capacity_workload.py   -> capacity_workload.csv
    scripts/run_capacity_demo.py       -> capacity_demo.csv
    scripts/run_f4_phaseA.py           -> f4_a1_ubatch_sweep.csv,
                                          f4_a3_flash_attn_capacity.csv,
                                          f4_a2_continuation_arms.csv

The `x_budget` / `device_over_budget` COLUMNS in those already-written CSVs are
stale. The MiB columns beside them are correct. Do not quote the ratio columns;
recompute as device_total_mib / 16384.

Some ratios in the prose were computed by hand against 16383 MiB and are
therefore already right, which is why the affected numbers are scattered rather
than uniform. Each is listed below.

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
--------------------
- Every MiB figure: KV, scratch, model, device totals, host store, free memory.
- Every KiB/token figure, including the 193 KiB/token headline and its ubatch
  dependence (136-194): these are slopes of MiB against C and never touched the
  budget.
- Every outcome: which configurations complete and which are refused.
- The B1 device-visible ceiling (81920-90112 cells) and its headroom arithmetic,
  which was done against 16383 MiB and is correct.
- All of Findings 1, 2, 3 and 5, which involve no memory ratio.

A NEAR MISS WORTH RECORDING
----------------------------
The bug first surfaced as a provenance assertion firing in the new capacity
figure: a run had been REFUSED at what appeared to be 0.997x the budget, below
the wall. The obvious reading was that the wall sits slightly below the advisory
value, and the figure was briefly rewritten to show the wall as a measured band
rather than a line -- a plausible-looking refinement to Finding 4 that was
entirely an artefact of the unit error. It survived about ten minutes, because
the "refinement" was strange enough to be worth checking rather than shipping.

With the correct budget the original claim is not merely restored but sharpened:
every completion sits below 16384 MiB and every refusal above it, with the
largest completion at 0.992x and the smallest refusal at 1.045x. The wall IS the
advisory budget.

Two lessons for the paper's own methodology section, which is about measurement
pitfalls and where this would sit honestly:
  1. A tool that prints MB and a tool that prints MiB will differ by 4.9%, which
     is small enough to look like a real effect and large enough to move a
     threshold.
  2. The assertion that caught it was checking a claim about the DATA, not about
     the code. An assertion that had only verified "the CSV parses" would have
     drawn the wrong figure silently.
