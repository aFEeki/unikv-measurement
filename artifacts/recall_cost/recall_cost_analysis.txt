========================================================================

control run  : recall_cost_steps_B_p1_ctrl.csv  (10751 decode steps)
NB the per-call CSV's `ttft_ms` column holds the per-decode-call wall
clock (llama_context::decode is bracketed by synchronize() on both ends
when UNIKV_LOG is set), not a time-to-first-token; the header name is a
legacy misnomer. Step 1 (prefill) is excluded throughout.
