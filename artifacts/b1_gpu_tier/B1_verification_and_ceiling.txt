B1 — interleaved verification and the device-visible ceiling
============================================================
2026-08-06. Harnesses: scripts/run_b1_interleaved.py, scripts/run_b1_ceiling.py
Data: stress_results/b1_interleaved_ab.csv, b1_device_tier_ceiling.csv
Logs: artifacts/b1_gpu_tier/{logs,ceiling_logs}/
Follows: artifacts/b1_gpu_tier/B1_triage.txt (bucket call (a))

Neither block is cooled. The interleaved one substitutes COUNTERBALANCING for
cooling, so its within-round differences are drift-immune while its absolute
levels are not protocol numbers. The ceiling block produces categorical outcomes
(completes / refused), which thermal state does not change.

PART 1 — THE PREMIUM AGAINST H2O HOLDS, AND IT IS THE TIGHTEST NUMBER HERE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three arms at C=1024, 1536 decode, -fa off, uninstrumented, in rotated order
(ABC, BCA, CAB, CBA), n=4 each.

  arm                        n    mean     sd     range
  p3 CPU-pinned            4    25.22   3.24   23.04 - 30.03
  p3 device-visible        4    36.60   1.66   34.87 - 38.22
  H2O                      4    39.45   2.16   37.36 - 41.65

  within-round paired differences:
    H2O vs device-visible      + 7.77%  +/- 2.08   (+10.1 +7.2 +8.5 +5.2)
    device-visible vs CPU-pin  +46.50%  +/- 15.41  (+25.9 +61.6 +44.5 +54.1)
    H2O vs CPU-pinned          +57.69%  +/- 14.39  (+38.7 +73.2 +56.8 +62.1)

The sub-10% exactness premium is CONFIRMED: +7.77% +/- 2.08, every round inside
[5.2, 10.1]. This is the number the extended version hangs on and it is the most
stable comparison in the set.

THE DEVICE-VISIBLE SPEEDUP IS NOT +24%, AND IS NOT A SINGLE NUMBER
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The triage smoke said +24%; counterbalanced measurement says +46.5% +/- 15.4.
The per-arm ranges explain the discrepancy and are the more interesting result:

  CPU-pinned      30.03 -> 23.04 across the session   (-23%, 30% spread)
  device-visible  37.81 -> 35.50                      ( -6%, 10% spread)
  H2O             41.65 -> 37.36                      (-10%, 11% spread)

The CPU-pinned arm is far more thermally sensitive than the other two, because
CPU work throttles harder than GPU work on this device. It is not merely slower;
it is less predictable. That also explains why the two comparisons have such
different stability: H2O against device-visible compares two GPU-resident arms
with similar thermal behaviour and lands at +/-2.08, while anything involving the
CPU-pinned arm inherits its throttling and lands at +/-15.

Consequence for reporting: the device-visible speedup should be given as a range
or measured cooled, not quoted as a point value. B2 pins it.

------------------------------------------------

  CPU-pinned           98304     12288       11311             0   22.71   completes
  CPU-pinned          131072     16384       11311             0   22.38   completes

Two clean facts:

1. The device-visible tier is charged in full against the device. Free memory
   falls by exactly the cap at every step, and the tier shows up as
   `unaccounted` because it sits outside the ggml scheduler's tracked
   categories. The ceiling is between 81920 and 90112 cells, i.e. 10.2-11.3 GiB
   of tier, which matches the headroom arithmetic (16383 - 4685 model - 128 KV -
   258 compute = 11312 MiB) essentially exactly.

2. The CPU-pinned tier is invisible to the device. Free memory is 11311 MiB at
   EVERY capacity, including a 16 GiB tier on a 24 GB machine, and there is no
   device-side ceiling at all. This is precisely what the pin buys, stated as a
   measurement rather than as a design intention.

Two details worth keeping:

- The failure is again GPU_OOM, not an allocation failure: 11264 MiB of tier
  ALLOCATES and is then refused at execution. Finding 4's central observation
  reproduces in a configuration it was not derived from, which is the strongest
  evidence for it so far.
- Unlike the ubatch sweep, there is no slow-then-refused band here: throughput is
  flat to the wall (37.89 at 2 GiB, 36.73 at 10 GiB, a 3% decline) and then the
  configuration simply stops working. The "approach the budget and it degrades"
  behaviour seen in the A1 sweep is specific to configurations where the
  COMPUTE SCRATCH is what grows; a large static buffer does not behave that way.

WHAT THE TRADE ACTUALLY IS
---------------------------
Putting the two halves together, device-visibility is not a free improvement and
it is not a small one either. It is a move along a curve:

  CPU-pinned tier      ~25 tok/s (thermally unstable, 30% session spread)
                       no device-side capacity limit; tier bounded only by host
                       RAM. Device working set stays O(C).

  device-visible tier  ~37 tok/s (stable, within 7.8% of H2O)
                       device-bounded at ~82000-90000 spilled cells. Device
                       working set becomes O(C + spill capacity).

And the sharp consequence for the paper's capacity story: at its largest working
capacity the device-visible configuration holds C=1024 resident plus 81920
spilled, about 82900 tokens of context. A fused upstream build reaches roughly
91000 tokens (A3, Phase A) with the whole context resident and no spill machinery
at all. So in device-visible mode exact retention does not merely lose its
capacity advantage over upstream -- it has slightly LESS reach than fused
upstream, while still being the slower way to get there.

The capacity argument therefore survives only in CPU-pinned mode, and the
throughput argument survives only in device-visible mode. They cannot be claimed
together, and which one is on the table is a configuration choice the paper has
to make explicit.

LIMITS
-------
- n=4 per arm, uncooled. Differences within rounds are drift-immune; levels are
  not. The CPU-pinned arm's value in particular is thermally state-dependent by
  30% and should not be quoted from this block.
- Ceiling bracketed between 81920 and 90112 cells, not bisected. A finer walk
  would place it to within a few thousand cells if the paper needs a single
  number rather than an interval.
- One C (1024). The ceiling is a function of total working set, so a larger
  resident window moves it down by the corresponding amount; that follows from
  the accounting but was not measured across C.
- Device-visible mode is Metal/unified-memory specific and meaningless on
  discrete memory, where the pin is doing real work.
